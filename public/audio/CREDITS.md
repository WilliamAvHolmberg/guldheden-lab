# Audio

All files are loudness-normalised (EBU R128) and re-encoded to MP3 (music 96 kbps stereo, effects 64 kbps mono).

| File | What | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `soft-1..3.mp3` | Soft-tempo songs (1–2 people on the stage, or someone on a bench) | Chosen by the Energy group (`Design-in-action.zip`) |
| `fast-1..3.mp3` | High-tempo songs (3+ people on the stage) | Chosen by the Energy group |
| `street.mp3` | Street ambience (nobody on the stage) | Chosen by the Energy group |
| `laugh.mp3` | Laughter when avatars talk | Chosen by the Energy group |
| `murmur.mp3` | Murmur of people talking while avatars talk | "Restaurant Ambience" by stephan, Wikimedia Commons, public domain: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Restaurant_ambience.ogg |
