Det interaktiva arkivet

A walkable simulation of the installation on Guldhedstorget, Gothenburg. An LED wall in the empty shopfronts turns everyone who steps up into a historical figure from the square that copies their movements. The more neighbours there are, the more colour, light and music.

W A S D walk · Shift run · drag to look · scroll to zoom
1–8 gestures · Space jump · F face the screen
Click the buttons on the pillar next to the screen to change the era
C webcam pose (MediaPipe) · K sensor view · V screen feed
Enter chatta – texten syns ovanför din figur · E sätt dig på en bänk
Följ de gula ljusslingorna i marken fram till skärmen
