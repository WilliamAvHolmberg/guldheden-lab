The Interactive Archive at Guldhedstorget

A walkable simulation of the installation on Guldhedstorget, Gothenburg. A freestanding LED wall out on the square, facing the big street, turns everyone who steps onto the stage into a historical figure from the square that copies their movements. The more neighbours there are, the more colour, light and music.
